Soluklan press kit

In one paragraph

Soluklan is a breathing exercise app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android that measures your breathing instead of only showing an animation. During a session the microphone follows your breath and scores how closely you kept the rhythm; afterwards a short report explains, in plain words, what went well and what to change next time. Sixteen techniques, from Box Breathing and 4-7-8 to Humming Bee and Kapalabhati, sit alongside nine programs that move forward with your results. With version 2.0, Soluklan can also measure your breathing while you lie back with your phone on your belly and your eyes closed, and lets you breathe together with a friend in real time. It is a relaxation and focus exercise app, not a medical device. Soluklan is made by an independent developer in Türkiye, with no ads and no tracking.

Features

Measurement

  • Breath tracking with the microphone. The app hears your exhales during a session. No audio is recorded: only the moment-to-moment sound level is worked out, on the device.
  • Lying-down breathing — New in 2.0. Lie back with the phone on your belly and close your eyes: the motion sensors measure your breathing without the microphone, quiet nasal breathing included.
  • A score for every session. Rhythm match, exhales heard, exhale length, the inhale, breath volume, how smooth the exhale was and whether you finished, with rhythm match weighing most.
  • Room measurement. The room's sound is measured before the session and again during it, so a door opening or the air conditioning switching off doesn't throw the measurement.
  • Holds measured. In techniques with a hold, such as 4-7-8 and Box Breathing, the app notices air slipping out; gradual holds that start short and grow are available to everyone in 2.0.
  • Your own pace — Improved in 2.0. A pace test finds your resonance pace to a tenth of a breath per minute, sitting, lying down or together with Apple Watch.
  • No microphone? No problem. Every session can be done with the microphone off; with lying-down breathing you still get a full measurement.

Sixteen techniques

Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Coherent Breathing, Physiological Sigh, Triangle Breathing, Extended Exhale, Deep and Slow, Energizing Breath, Alternate Nostril, Pursed Lip, Humming Bee, Ocean Breath (Ujjayi), Cooling Breath, Left Nostril Breath, Right Nostril Breath and Kapalabhati. Each one has a learn page with how to do it, common slips and a guided practice step. Techniques open through use, not through payment. Eleven of them can be done lying down (New in 2.0).

Programs

Nine programs that run for weeks: 28-Day Sighing, 4-Week Box Breathing, Balance in Three Steps, Bedtime Routine, Breathing Basics, Find Your Own Pace, Big Day Prep (for an exam, a presentation, an interview and many other occasions), Long Breath (a longer, slower exhale over six weeks, with a weekly measurement) and 28-Day Humming Bee. Each day is set up on its own: lengths grow after strong sessions and ease off after a hard day, special days (silent, eyes closed, unguided ending) appear along the way, and a program map shows the path. Improved in 2.0: a sky-coloured time dial for choosing the program time, and lying-down days where they fit (Bedtime Routine, Breathing Basics, Long Breath).

Reports and summaries

  • Session report — Improved in 2.0: what happened, where it happened and how to fix it. Every suggestion comes with a short how-to and a button that opens the right place, and the report compares the session with your usual results.
  • Report in parts for sessions over two minutes, so you can see whether you struggled at the start or drifted at the end.
  • Breath chart with marked moments (Advanced level) and heart rate chart in shared sessions with Apple Watch.
  • Personal summary (common slips, technique comparison, your best time of day) and weekly summaries with tags, filters, search and pinning.
  • Program summary when a program ends.
  • Sleep score from your sleep and heart rate data in Health, compared only with your own usual nights. Off by default; processed in memory on the device.
  • Share cards for a session, a streak or a milestone, drawn on the device.

Streak and milestones

A daily streak, streak shields that cover a missed day automatically (up to three), streak repair when it breaks (redesigned in 2.0: your waiting streak, the days left and each session's progress at a glance), an end-of-day countdown (New in 2.0 on iOS as a Live Activity), milestones on the calendar that you can name and colour, and achievements.

Friends — New in 2.0

Optional accounts (email, Sign in with Apple, Google, or your username), friends by link, QR code, six-digit code or username, gentle nudges and break suggestions, programs followed together and Breathe together: a live session for two where each of you sees the other's breath. No leaderboards and no rankings; blocking and reporting are built in.

Apple Watch

A watch app where the rhythm comes as taps on the wrist and your heart rate is measured, and a shared session: the phone hears your breath while the watch reads your heart rate, with both in one report. Improved in 2.0: a redesigned watch app that follows the time of day, a Start complication, and heart rate in lying-down sessions.

Widgets, Live Activities and shortcuts

Streak and Start widgets from small to extra large, Lock Screen widgets, a Quick Start widget and Control Center button (iOS 18), a Live Activity during the session, Home Screen icon shortcuts and Siri shortcuts. New in 2.0: Glow widgets with a ring for the week and a Friends widget. On Android, a streak widget and an end-of-day countdown notification.

Make it yours

  • Three interface levels: Simple (only Start on the home screen), Standard and Advanced.
  • A line above Start. A single sentence above the Start button changes every time you come back to the home screen and is chosen for you: a fact from your own sessions, a gentle tip for the technique you struggle with, where you are in your program, how close the next milestone is, a nudge to try a technique that suits this hour, a short note on a special day, what a friend is up to, and much more. Tap the technique it mentions and it is selected for you.
  • Settings in sections with four personal suggestions at the top (Improved in 2.0).
  • Dynamic appearance that follows the time of day, the season and special days, or Light, Dark and System. Special days change with your language and region: each user sees the celebrations of their own culture, such as national days, Christmas, Lunar New Year or Ramadan, with the right date for their country. New in 2.0: a glowing breathing circle that takes on the day's theme, session scenes, and new themes such as a Ramadan theme for the whole month.
  • Session sounds and notification sounds: thirteen sound sets for the guide during a session and twelve for notifications.
  • Long sessions of up to twenty minutes, depending on the technique, with a soft interval chime.
  • Sessions with the screen off in most techniques (New in 2.0), with Pause and Resume on the Lock Screen.
  • Battery saving when the battery is low or the device is warm; measurement and score stay the same.
  • iPad layout and support for the foldable iPhone Duo, including a split session screen when half-folded.

Health and Health Connect

Mindful minutes can be written to Apple Health or Health Connect (Android 14 and later); the switch starts off. Heart rate is read from Apple Watch in shared sessions. Sleep and heart rate data can be read for the sleep score.

Reminders

Reminders that vary their time and technique instead of repeating the same message, quiet hours, a streak reminder and a reminder at your program time, set with the new time dial (Improved in 2.0).

Accessibility

  • VoiceOver labels on buttons, and VoiceOver announcements of each phase during a session.
  • Larger text up to the accessibility sizes (AX3); increased contrast with the system setting.
  • Reduce Motion stops decorative motion (the breathing circle keeps moving, because it shows the rhythm).
  • Voice Control; information never relies on colour alone; dark interface, including on Apple Watch.
  • Sessions can be followed without looking: haptic taps and tones on the phone, taps on the wrist on Apple Watch, and, new in 2.0, lying-down sessions on a dimmed screen guided by tones.
  • A Simple interface level, and sessions without the microphone.

What's new in 2.0

Soluklan 2.0 is the biggest update since launch.

Lying-down breathing

Lie back, place your phone screen-up on your belly and close your eyes. The motion sensors follow the rise and fall of your belly, so breathing is measured without the microphone, quiet nasal breathing included. The screen dims right down to a simple breathing circle and tones guide each phase. At the end, a short card shows how your belly breathing changed through the session. It works in eleven techniques, including 4-7-8, Coherent Breathing and Box Breathing, and switches on by itself when the phone lies flat and moves with your breath. Motion data stays on the device and needs no permission. On iPad, lying-down breathing is hidden by default.

Apple Watch, heart rate and your own pace

  • Heart rate from Apple Watch joins lying-down sessions: breath sound, belly movement and heart rate in one session, with the guidance as taps on the wrist.
  • The pace test can be done lying down or together with the watch, and gives your resonance pace to a tenth of a breath per minute (for example 5.6), within a 4.5 to 6.5 range.
  • A redesigned watch app and a watch complication that shows your streak and opens Start.

Breathe with friends

Accounts are optional and arrive with 2.0 (email, Sign in with Apple, Google; sign in with your username too). With an account you can add friends by link, QR code or six-digit code, or find them by username. Friends can see whether you breathed today, your streak, your weekly minutes and your current program, each of which you can hide; scores are never shared. You can nudge a friend, suggest a break, congratulate them, follow a program together and see when a friend is breathing right now. Breathe together starts a live session for two: a shared waiting room, a 3-2-1 countdown, and each of you sees the other's breath on screen. There are no leaderboards and no rankings. Blocking, reporting and muting are built in, and social features are off for children under 13.

Reports that show you how

Every suggestion in the report now comes with a short how-to, and a button that opens the right place: the technique, its learn page, a setting or a program. Lying-down sessions get their own measures.

Sessions with the screen off

In most techniques the session keeps going when the phone locks, guided by tones or by Apple Watch, with Pause and Resume on the Lock Screen. The report notes how many minutes were done with the screen off.

Keeping the habit

  • An end-of-day countdown in a Live Activity shows how long is left until midnight when your streak is waiting. It respects Focus.
  • A redesigned streak repair, and partial sessions now add up towards the day.
  • A discovery week for new users: a different easy technique every day, ending with the one that suits you.
  • A short setup path and an update tour.

A new look

The whole app is redesigned: a new colour, a glowing breathing circle that takes on the day's theme, screens that flow into one another from Start to the end of a session, Glow widgets with a ring for the week, a Friends widget, a sky-coloured time dial for setting program times, and Settings organised into sections with personal suggestions. New themes: morning light, a Ramadan theme for the whole month, and cultural days such as Christmas, Advent, Easter (Western and Orthodox), Halloween, Thanksgiving and Día de Muertos, depending on language and region.

Four new languages

Korean, Polish, Thai and Vietnamese, for 16 languages in total.

What's next

Soluklan keeps growing after 2.0. Planned for the coming months:

  • More for friends: new ways to support each other, such as giving a friend one of your streak shields when they run out, and more to do together.
  • Parent-managed accounts for children: a parent account to set up and manage a child's account, with the child's privacy in mind.
  • Soluklan Plus and a family plan: more programs, techniques and personal insights for those who want to go further, while the breathing exercises themselves stay free.
  • Wear OS: the Apple Watch experience, including shared sessions with heart rate, on Android watches.

And much more.

Privacy

  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. The app uses only Apple's and Google's own frameworks.
  • No audio recordings. The microphone runs only during a session and only the sound level is processed. The one exception is an audio sample you can choose to share, under separate permission, to help improve measurement; it is deleted once sent.
  • Your answers stay on your device. Age range and health notes are used to adjust techniques and warnings and are never sent anywhere.
  • Your history is yours. Without an account, sessions sync only in your own iCloud (on Android, your own Google backup). Soluklan works without an internet connection.
  • Accounts are optional. With an account, your session history (numbers only), streak and settings sync through Soluklan's server; health information does not. Friends see only the few values you allow; in Breathe together the server passes on live breath events without storing them.
  • Anonymous sharing only with consent, and you can delete what you shared from Settings.
  • Health data is processed on the device. Sleep and heart rate data for the sleep score is processed in memory and isn't written to disk, backed up or sent.

About the developer

Soluklan is made by an independent developer in Türkiye. It has grown, one small release at a time, from eleven techniques in September 2026 to an app on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android in sixteen languages with version 2.0.

Health note

Soluklan offers relaxation and focus exercises. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
